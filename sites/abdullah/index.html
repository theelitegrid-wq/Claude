Questions people ask first.

Will the AI agent say something wrong to my customers?

The agent only answers from the information we give it, such as your services, prices and policies. When it isn't sure, it hands the conversation to you instead of guessing. You test it before launch and can read every conversation afterwards.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I set everything up and connect it to the tools you already use. You get a short walkthrough and a simple way to update prices, hours and FAQs.

What do I pay each month?

The care plan covers hosting, AI usage within normal volume, monitoring and monthly improvements. Very high message volume is quoted separately, and you'll know about it in advance.

Can it work in Arabic or Urdu?

Yes. The agent can reply in the language the customer writes in, and we can set a main language for your business.

Who owns the website and the data?

You do. Your domain, your content and your customer data stay in accounts you own.
