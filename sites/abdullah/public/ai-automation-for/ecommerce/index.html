AI automation for e-commerce stores

The problem

Where e-commerce stores lose customers

"Where is my order?" fills the inbox

Order status questions make up a large share of support tickets and take time from harder cases.

Shoppers leave with unanswered questions

Questions about size, ingredients or delivery go unanswered, so the visitor leaves.

Abandoned carts with no follow-up

Shoppers who added items but didn't buy rarely hear from you again.

Returns handled by hand

Every return means back-and-forth emails, labels and refunds.

The solution

What your AI agent does

  • Answers order tracking, shipping and returns questions instantly using your store data
  • Recommends products based on what the shopper tells it, like a good shop assistant
  • Starts returns and exchanges and sends return labels automatically
  • Recovers abandoned carts with personal email and SMS follow-ups
  • Hands complex or upset customers to your support team with the full conversation
  • Collects product feedback and reviews after delivery

FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI agent work with Shopify?

Yes. It can look up orders, products and customers in Shopify and answer from live data.

Can it process refunds?

It can start returns and send labels. Refunds can be automatic for simple cases or approved by your team, depending on your rules.

Will it recommend the right products?

It recommends from your catalogue based on what the customer tells it, and you control which products and rules it uses.
