How an AI agent is different from an old chatbot

Old chatbots followed fixed menus and broke as soon as a customer asked something unexpected. A modern AI agent understands normal language, keeps track of the conversation and can take actions: checking your calendar, booking a slot, updating your CRM or sending a message to your team.

It also knows when to stop. When a question falls outside what it has been taught, the agent hands the conversation to you instead of guessing.

FAQ

Common questions

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

We recommend the agent introduces itself as an assistant. Customers mostly care about getting a fast, correct answer, and the agent hands over to a person whenever they ask.

Can the AI agent give wrong answers?

It only answers from the information we give it, and it is set up to hand over when it is unsure. You test it before launch and can read every conversation.

Which channels can the agent work on?

Your website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook messages, SMS and phone calls. Most businesses start with one or two channels and add more later.

How much does an AI agent cost to run?

Setup starts at $2,500. The monthly care plan starts at $300 and covers hosting, normal AI usage, monitoring and monthly improvements.
