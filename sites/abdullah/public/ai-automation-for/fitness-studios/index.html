The problem

Where gyms and fitness studios lose customers

Trial sign-ups who never show up

People book a trial, get busy, and never come in.

No follow-up after the trial

Without a timely message, trial members drift away instead of joining.

Members cancel without a conversation

By the time someone cancels, it's too late to win them back.

Class and pricing questions all day

Front desk staff answer the same schedule and membership questions repeatedly.

The solution

What your AI agent does

  • Books free trials and class spots from your website, Instagram and WhatsApp
  • Sends reminders before the trial and personal follow-ups after it
  • Answers class schedule, pricing and membership questions
  • Flags members whose attendance drops and sends a friendly check-in
  • Fills cancelled class spots from a waitlist
  • Asks members for reviews and referrals at the right time

FAQ

Common questions

Can the AI book into Mindbody or Glofox?

Many gym systems can be connected so bookings appear in your schedule automatically. If not, the agent books into a shared calendar your team confirms.

How does it help keep members?

It watches for signs like fewer visits and sends a personal check-in or offer before the member decides to cancel.

Can it sell memberships?

It can explain options, answer questions and send a payment link. You decide which offers it is allowed to make.
