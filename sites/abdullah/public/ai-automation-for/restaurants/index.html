The problem

Where restaurants lose customers

Phones ring during service

Staff either ignore the phone or leave guests waiting at the table to answer it.

Booking requests stuck in DMs

Reservations sent on Instagram and WhatsApp go unanswered until the next day.

Private events slip away

Large, valuable event enquiries need quick, detailed replies that a busy manager can't always give.

Few reviews from happy guests

Most happy guests never leave a review unless someone asks at the right moment.

The solution

What your AI agent does

  • Takes reservations by website chat, Instagram, WhatsApp and phone, straight into your booking system
  • Answers questions about the menu, dietary options, parking and opening hours
  • Records allergies and special occasions on the booking for the kitchen and floor team
  • Captures private event details such as date, group size and budget, and sends them to your events manager
  • Sends reminders to cut no-shows and frees tables that won't be used
  • Asks guests for a Google review after their visit

FAQ

Common questions

Can the AI book into my reservation system?

Yes for most popular systems. If yours can't be connected, the agent books into a shared calendar or sends confirmed details to your team.

How does it handle allergies?

It records allergies on the booking and passes them to the kitchen. For detailed allergen questions it uses your allergen information or hands over to a person.

Can it answer the phone during service?

Yes. An AI voice host can answer calls, take reservations and answer common questions so your staff can stay with guests.
