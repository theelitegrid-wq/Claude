FAQ

Common questions

Why do prices say "from"?

Projects vary in the number of channels, tools and pages involved. After a free call you get a fixed quote for your exact project.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, AI usage within normal volume, monitoring, fixes and monthly improvements. Very high message volume is quoted separately and agreed in advance.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Larger projects are usually split into a deposit and a final payment at launch. Ask on your call.

Can I cancel the care plan?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Your website, accounts and data stay yours.
