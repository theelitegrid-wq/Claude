FAQ

Common questions

Can the AI agent answer questions about specific listings?

Yes. It reads your listing details, such as price, size, features and availability, and answers from them. Anything it doesn't know is passed to the listing agent.

Does it work with property portals?

Leads from most portals arrive by email or through an integration, and the agent can reply to them instantly in the same thread or by text.

Will it replace my agents?

No. It handles first replies, qualification and scheduling so your agents spend their time with serious buyers and sellers.
