# First client today: Abdullah Automations

The goal for today is one paying client, not a perfect business. That means a small offer that's easy to say yes to, many conversations, and fast follow-up.

---

## 1. The offer (use this today, not your $1,500+ packages)

**"Missed-Call Rescue": live in 72 hours, $497 one-time**

- When a call is missed, the customer gets a text within seconds
- An AI assistant replies, answers basic questions and books them in
- Every lead is saved in one place you can check on your phone
- First month of running costs included, then $97/month (cancel any time)
- **Guarantee:** if it doesn't win you at least one new enquiry in the first 30 days, you get your money back

Why this offer: it's cheap enough to say yes to on the first call, the value is easy to see (every missed call is lost money), and it leads naturally to your bigger packages later.

**Founding-client bonus (only for your first 3 clients):** 30% off any website or AI agent package within 90 days, in exchange for a short testimonial.

---

## 2. Who to contact (in this order)

1. **People who already know you.** Friends, family and old colleagues who own a business or know someone who does. This is the fastest route to a first sale.
2. **Local service businesses that take bookings by phone.** Dentists, salons, plumbers, electricians, real estate agents, clinics, gyms. Find them on Google Maps: search "[business type] near me" and look for:
   - few or old reviews
   - a dated website, or no website
   - no online booking
3. **Freelance marketplaces.** Upwork and Fiverr buyers who are already looking for "AI chatbot", "n8n automation", "AI agent" or "website".

**Target for today: 40 personal messages, 5 calls, 1 sale.**

## 5. On the call (15 minutes)

1. **Ask (5 min):** How do customers contact you? What happens after hours? How many calls do you miss a week? What is one customer worth to you?
2. **Show (5 min):** Open one of your demo sites at abdullahautomations.com/work/ and book an appointment through the assistant, live on screen.
3. **Close (5 min):** "Based on what you said, missing [X] calls a week at [$Y] each is about [$Z] a month. This is $497 once, with a money-back guarantee. Shall I send the payment link so I can start today?"

If they hesitate: "What would you need to see to feel comfortable?" Then answer that, or offer to start with a smaller first step.
