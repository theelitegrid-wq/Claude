Dental clinics

Your front desk is busy with patients in the chair, so calls go to voicemail and new patients book somewhere else. An AI receptionist answers every call and message, books appointments into your schedule and reminds patients so fewer chairs sit empty.

Real estate agents

In real estate the first agent to reply usually wins the client. An AI agent answers every portal, website and social enquiry in seconds, qualifies the buyer or seller, and books viewings straight into your calendar while you are out showing homes.

AI automation for real estate agents

Restaurants

When the dining room is full, nobody has time to answer the phone or reply to Instagram. An AI host takes reservations on every channel, answers menu and allergy questions, captures private event enquiries and asks happy guests for reviews.

AI automation for restaurants

E-commerce stores

Online shoppers expect instant answers at any hour. An AI shopping assistant tracks orders, handles returns, recommends the right products and recovers abandoned carts, so your support inbox shrinks and more visitors turn into customers.

AI automation for e-commerce stores

Law firms

Potential clients often call several firms and hire the first one that responds properly. An AI intake assistant answers every enquiry, screens it by case type, books consultations with the right lawyer and collects documents before the first meeting.

AI automation for law firms

Gyms and fitness studios

Most studios lose members in two places: trials that never convert and members who quietly stop coming. An AI assistant books trials, follows up until people join, answers schedule questions and flags members who are likely to cancel.

AI automation for gyms and fitness studios

Home service businesses

When you are on a job you can't answer the phone, and a missed call is usually a lost job. An AI assistant texts back every missed call in seconds, books the job, follows up on every quote and asks for a review when the work is done.

Salons and spas

Most salon bookings now start in a DM. An AI assistant replies to Instagram and WhatsApp messages instantly, books clients into your calendar, fills last-minute cancellations from a waitlist and reminds clients when it is time to come back.
