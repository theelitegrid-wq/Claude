FAQ

Common questions

Can the AI book into Fresha, Vagaro or Booksy?

Many salon booking systems can be connected. Where they can't, the agent sends confirmed bookings to your team or uses a shared calendar.

Can it recommend the right stylist or treatment?

Yes. It uses your service list and stylist skills to suggest the right option and price.

How does it fill cancellations?

When a slot opens, it messages clients on your waitlist and books the first one to accept.
