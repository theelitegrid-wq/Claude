FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It collects information, answers general questions about your firm and books consultations. Legal questions are always passed to a lawyer.

Can it screen out enquiries we don't handle?

Yes. It asks your intake questions and politely refers enquiries outside your practice areas or location.

Is client information kept confidential?

The assistant runs on accounts you control and collects only what you need for intake. It can be set up to follow your confidentiality and data policies.
