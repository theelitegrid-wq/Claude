The problem

Where dental clinics lose customers

Missed calls during clinic hours

When reception is checking someone in, the phone rings out. Many new patients call the next clinic on the list instead of leaving a message.

No-shows and late cancellations

An empty chair is lost revenue you can't get back. Manual reminder calls take hours each week.

The same questions all day

Do you take my insurance? How much is a cleaning? Are you open Saturday? Your team answers them again and again.

After-hours emergencies

Patients in pain search at night and on weekends and book whoever answers first.

The solution

What your AI agent does

  • Answers calls, website chat and WhatsApp 24/7 and books straight into your practice calendar
  • Sends confirmations and reminders, and offers cancelled slots to patients on a waitlist
  • Answers insurance, pricing and opening-hours questions from your own information
  • Triages emergencies and offers the next urgent slot, or alerts the on-call dentist
  • Sends new-patient forms before the visit so check-in is quick
  • Asks happy patients for a Google review after their appointment

FAQ

Common questions

Can the AI receptionist book into my dental software?

In most cases, yes. Many practice systems can be connected directly or through a booking tool. If yours can't, the agent books into a shared calendar that your team confirms.

Is patient information kept private?

The agent only collects what is needed to book, uses accounts you own and can be set up to follow your data protection rules. Clinical questions are always passed to your team.

Will it handle emergency calls?

Yes. It recognises urgent cases such as pain, swelling or a broken tooth, offers the next emergency slot and can alert the on-call dentist.
